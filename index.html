From brief to buildable concept in three steps

  1. 1

    Describe your brief

    Plot, planning rules, size, rooms, kitchen, style and what matters most to you. Type in the plot's size, or draw your lot on a map. Six short steps, about three minutes.

  2. 2

    Compare four designs

    We lay out 36 to 108 house plans, and hundreds more when the plot is tight. We reject any that break a planning rule, score the rest on daylight, privacy, flow and cost, and keep the four that fit you best. If nothing fits your plot as briefed, we show buildable designs with one tested change, and tell you why.

  3. 3

    Explore and share

    Walk through each design in 3D, follow the sun across the day, then export plans and a BIM model for your architect.

Measured floor plans

An indicative build cost based on floor area, storeys, finishes and extras like a pool.
